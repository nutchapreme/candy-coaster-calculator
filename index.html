Units:

Block clearance times

Each value is the minimum time between consecutive trains entering that block. Include occupancy, braking/release, and required clearance. These are test assumptions, not validated safe values. The block count must equal or exceed the number of trains plus one.

Operating constraints and interpretation

Dispatch interval: The largest of (full cycle time / train count), station occupancy, and the maximum user-entered block clearance interval. This is a theoretical scheduling lower bound, not a safety validation. Actual operation may be slower due to interactions between blocks, queues, switches, sensors, and control logic.

Track length: This calculator does not establish a minimum track length for multiple trains. Train length, physical block lengths, stopping distance, sensor latency, and stopping/restarting capability must be designed and tested separately.

Physics: Ideal bottom speed assumes a vertical drop, and the energy-adjusted estimate applies a user-specified retained fraction to gravitational potential energy only. Turn acceleration assumes an unbanked flat turn at the estimated bottom speed, not a measured train acceleration. Powered launches can invalidate gravity-only comparisons.

8-hour output: Estimated throughput uses a user-assumed uptime percentage and full trains. It does not prove an eight-hour endurance requirement.
